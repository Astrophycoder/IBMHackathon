# MORETOGETHER — Product and Scope

## Product identity
**MORETOGETHER — More Opportunities. Greater Together.**

A community-powered opportunity, livelihood and contribution platform connecting people, businesses, startups, NGOs and community groups through paid work, products/services, volunteering, mentorship, skill exchange and collaboration. It is not merely a job portal.

People should be able to participate without formal degrees, professional job titles, polished CVs or certificates. Informal and hobby skills are valid capabilities.

## Intended users
Older adults, low-income communities, people with limited literacy/digital literacy, homemakers and unpaid caregivers, unemployed youth, people with disabilities, neurodivergent people, small businesses, informal workers, home-based businesses, artists, makers, NGOs, volunteers and community groups. Do not add child-focused features in this version.

## Two-way opportunity economy
1. Organisation posts a requirement → people apply/offer.
2. Person lists skills/products/services → others discover/contact.
3. Person or business posts a need → platform finds potential providers.
4. Platform identifies complementary skills and suggests collaborations.

Examples: local shop needs poster/website/social media design; bag maker needs customers or product photography; tailor and designer collaborate; home-based maker finds buyers; hobby coder helps a local business.

## Page requirements

### Home
Navbar (logo, search, notifications, profile); welcome banner; search prompt “What are you looking for?”; quick actions (Find a Job, Find an Internship, Hire Talent, Find Volunteers, Offer My Skills, Find a Mentor); explore categories; recommended opportunities; nearby events/community activities; TogetherPoints balance. ### Opportunity Hub
Categories: Jobs, Internships, Freelance Projects, Startup Requirements, Business Requirements, Creative Projects, Event Opportunities, Volunteering, Mentorship, Collaborations. Support paid employment, part-time/flexible micro-work, hobby earning, home-based work, product selling, service offering, skill exchange and unpaid volunteering. Include search/filters, cards, post requirement, saved opportunities, applications and talent shortlisting. Filters: keyword, category, skills, location/remote, budget/payment, paid/unpaid, availability, experience, accessibility compatibility and sort. Posting supports jobs, services, freelancers, creative collaborators, volunteers, products and mentors.

### Talent Hub
Developers, Designers, Singers, Musicians, Dancers, Photographers, Video Editors, Artists, Writers, Teachers & Tutors, Digital Marketers, Event Organizers, Volunteers, Mentors, Other Skills. Also tailoring, cooking/baking, crafts, handmade products, gardening, repairs, home services, product photography, decoration, translation and DIY. Include talent cards, skill search, optional portfolio, availability, match explanation, invite/contact and complementary-skill discovery. No professional portfolio/education requirement.

### Community Hub
NGO Connect, Elderly Care, FoodShare, Women Empowerment, Education Support, Disability Inclusion, Environment & Animal Welfare, Clothing & Book Donations, Farmer Support, Mental Wellness, Home & Transport Assistance, Community Events, Fundraising and Digital Literacy. Include NGO profiles, campaigns, volunteer opportunities, donation listings, community requests and nearby initiatives. Mental wellness is community-support discovery, not diagnosis or therapy.

### Exchange Hub
TogetherPoints dashboard, earn/redeem points, skill exchange, time bank, free tutoring, mentorship exchange, shared resources, community gift pool, achievements/badges and transaction history. Keep real money separate from points.

### AI Assistant
Help find jobs/talent, match skills/requirements, recommend opportunities, identify skill gaps, build project teams, draft requirements from user requests, voice search and multilingual support. Also hobby earning, finding customers, complementary collaborators and local business needs. Clearly distinguish deterministic results from model-generated text. If no model is configured, use normal search/forms rather than fake AI responses.

### My Activity
Applications, posted requirements, invitations, projects, volunteer activities, events, donations, learning sessions, skill exchanges and rewards. Add products, services, orders/requests, earnings, payment acknowledgements, collaborations, completed work, feedback and reviews.

### Messages
One-to-one and organisation conversations; opportunity and collaboration discussions; attachments if supported; read state/unread counts; report/block and privacy controls.

### Notifications
Matching opportunities, application status, invitations, messages, volunteer reminders, contribution verification, points changes, redemption updates, payment acknowledgements and collaboration invitations. Include preferences, read/unread and history.

### My Profile
Personal information, education (optional), skills, interests, experience including informal experience, offers, needs, optional portfolio/certificates, availability, applications, contributions, points and achievements. Add products/services, preferred work type, payment preference, home/remote/in-person preference, accessibility preferences, language preferences, profile visibility and multiple roles.

## Money and TogetherPoints
Cash and UPI are direct arrangements between users. Initial version does not process payments, hold funds, provide escrow or operate an internal wallet. Parties agree amount/deliverable; payer may mark payment sent; receiver may acknowledge receipt. Receiver acknowledgement is not independent verification. Statuses may include pending, payer-marked-paid, receiver-acknowledged, disputed, cancelled and refunded (only when appropriately confirmed). Never collect UPI PINs, OTPs or bank credentials.

TogetherPoints are non-cash, non-transferable and non-cashable. Use an append-only ledger. Contributions require an authorised verification path. AI must not create, transfer or redeem points. Essential aid must not be conditional on volunteering or points.

## Accessibility and adaptive interface
Use user-selected, combinable preferences: text size, contrast, font/spacing, reduced motion, focus/reduced-clutter mode, voice input/output, language and captions. Never infer diagnoses or disabilities. Do not hide essential features. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA practices; do not claim compliance without an audit.
